## D-03 Standalone timelapse without a worker or paid source (agreed in principle; prototype works)
**Question asked by the owner:** can the timelapse run without a Cloudflare Worker and without paid sources?

**How tonguesfromspace does it** (read in `worker/worker.js`, `js/03-imagery.js`): the page calls a Worker that holds Copernicus Data Space OAuth client id/secret (up to 15 accounts), forwards Sentinel Hub Statistical API requests (scene scoring by SCL cloud/snow) and Process API requests (rendering), and caches in KV. Needs secrets, quota and a paid Cloudflare plan. **Not reusable for GfS.**

**Alternative checked 2026-11-03:** Element 84 *Earth Search* (public STAC, Sentinel-2 L2A as Cloud Optimized GeoTIFFs on the public AWS bucket `sentinel-cogs`).
- `POST https://earth-search.aws.element84.com/v1/search` answers 200 with `access-control-allow-origin: *` (curl, Origin header set).
- A COG asset (`.../TCI.tif`) answers byte-range requests (206) with `Access-Control-Allow-Origin: *`, `Accept-Ranges: bytes`.
- Result: a **static page in the browser** can search scenes and read only the needed pixels of a polygon (e.g. with geotiff.js), no key, no server. This could be one self-contained HTML file hosted on GitHub Pages, opened from the Results page with the polygon passed in the URL.

**Prototype result (2026-11-03):** `index.html` works in headless Chromium: Borneo (5.85 N) and Alps (46.5 N) polygons produce real frames, with scene choice and composites as in D-04. Pages, scenes and the Earth Search STAC were read live; nothing is cached or stored.

**Not yet verified:** terms of use / licence of Earth Search and the bucket (to read, Q-03); the Streamlit side (how to pass a polygon from the Results map to a link, Q-04); performance of reading many scenes in-browser; whether Sentinel-2 imagery is enough for small polygons (population polygons in the example are about 5 to 20 km across, fine at 10 m).

**Alternatives:** (a) Copernicus Browser deep link per polygon (no code, but no timelapse of our own, user must use its UI); (b) Sentinel Hub EO Browser link (same); (c) Google Earth Engine timelapse (needs an account, not "no key"); (d) own worker with CDSE credentials (needs secrets and quota: rejected by the constraints).

Stop-gap link to the Copernicus Browser: rejected by the owner (D-02).

## D-04 Scene choice per year (implemented in `index.html`, 2026-11-03; to be reviewed)
1. **Season** (the "relevant summer"): northern hemisphere June to September; southern December to March (season named after the year it ends in); **tropics (|latitude| < 23.5): there is no summer**, so the three months with the lowest mean tile cloud cover (`eo:cloud_cover` of all catalogue scenes over the polygon, all years) are used, the same months every year so frames are comparable. User can override the months on the page or with `from`/`to` in the link. *Assumption to confirm with the leader: Q-06. The tile cloud cover is a heuristic, not a climate record.*
2. **Single scene first:** scenes of the same day (several MGRS tiles) are merged; up to 10 days per year, lowest tile cloud first, are scored by the **SCL** band inside the polygon only; bad = no data, defective/saturated (1), cloud shadow (3), cloud (8, 9), thin cirrus (10); snow/ice (11) counts as clear. The scene with the least bad pixels is taken if **at most 2 %** are bad. (First try 5 %: visible clouds remained in the Borneo test, so 2 %.)
3. **Fallback composite:** otherwise the **per-pixel median** of the clear pixels of up to 12 best days in the same season window; pixels with no clear value stay dark grey and the share is reported. This is the "quarterly mosaic" of the owner's rule; for N/S hemisphere the window is the 4-month summer, not exactly a calendar quarter. *Question whether that is acceptable: Q-07.*
4. Pictures are the delivered **TCI** (true colour) product, cut to the polygon area, never recoloured. Resolution: 10 m pixel at best, at most 800 px per side (coarser overviews are read for large polygons).
5. First year **2017**: Earth Search has no L2A over Borneo in 2016 (search returned 0 scenes on 2026-11-03); 2017 is sparse there (37 scenes in the whole year).
6. The year range stops at the previous year unless the current season has ended.

Known limits of the prototype: cloud shadow outside SCL's detection can remain; a polygon spanning two UTM zones is not handled (each scene is read in its own CRS, it works, but not tested); heavy polygons (> 100 km across) are read from coarse overviews and are slow; no download/export yet; tested only in headless Chromium, not Safari/Firefox/phone.

## D-06 Fixes found while testing the wire (2026-11-03)
- **Search robustness:** the first wire test failed with "Failed to fetch" (CORS error in the console) on the tropics catalogue search. Earth Search answers some requests in a burst with an error that carries no CORS header; `stacPost` now retries up to 4 times with a growing pause. The cause was not established beyond that (curl bursts of 12 requests all returned 200), so it is a workaround, not a diagnosis.
- **Paging:** a year can have more than 200 scenes over a polygon (2018 over Sabah: 243); the page follows the API's `next` links (max 10 pages), de-duplicates by scene id and stops when a page brings nothing new. The API's page tokens were seen to step backwards in date between pages; results were still complete by id.
- **Tropics catalogue search** asks only for id, date and cloud cover (`fields`), to keep the answers small.
- **Season no longer depends on the years shown:** the tropical clearest three months were first computed from the years on screen, so the same polygon gave Mar to May for 2019 to 2021 and Jul to Sep for 2022 to 2023. It now always uses every full year since 2017 (D-04.1 amended). Also seen: for the mock `pop_3`, 2022 and 2023 had no single clear scene and used composites of 10 and 9 days (0.1 % and 0.0 % of the polygon left without a clear pixel).

## D-08 Faster scoring, loading display, saved pictures, year range (owner requests, 2026-11-03)
- **Year range, where it comes from:** *(first version, replaced by D-10: a fixed `FIRST_YEAR = 2017` that had only been checked over Borneo)*. The last year is the latest one whose season has ended (a tropical Mar to May season is over by 2026-11-03, a Jun to Sep season too). Both are now **selectable on the page** (Years x to y) or by `y0` / `y1` in the link, and the page says where they come from.
- **Faster scoring** (measured on the mock `pop_3`, Mar to May 2022 to 2024, three fresh browser contexts each, `from`/`to` given so the season search is excluded): old 34 s and 49 s, new 22 s and 24 s for three years. Two runs each, so "about 1.5 to 2 times faster", not a precise figure. Changes: (1) the cloud mask is read for **scoring** on a coarse grid (60 m pixels, at most 300 px, coarse overview of the SCL file) instead of the 10 m output grid; the full-resolution mask is only read for the days of a composite; (2) days are scored in **batches of 4** by tile cloud, stopping at the first batch with a clear day (at most 12 days, was 10 all at once); (3) **3 years are built in parallel**; (4) composites use at most **8 days** (was 12). Scene picks on the Alps test were unchanged (same GIF size as before), so the coarse scoring did not change which scenes were chosen there; this has not been compared on other polygons.
- **Loading display:** a progress bar and headline "Loading: n of N years finished, k in progress, m from saved pictures", one chip per year (waiting, searching, scoring x/y, composite, ready, saved, none), chips of finished years can be clicked, the picture area appears with the first finished year, and the final line says how many seconds it took.
- **Saved pictures per user:** finished years are stored in the browser's IndexedDB (key: version, hash of the polygon rounded to 4 decimals, months, year; also the tropics season search). Not shared between users or devices; lost if the user clears site data or uses a private window (the page then simply works without). Time to live 30 days (1 day for "no picture" results), at most 120 years kept, oldest dropped first. `CACHE_VERSION` in `index.html` must be raised whenever scoring or rendering changes. Link "forget saved pictures" in the footer. Tested: second open of the same population took 0 s (all chips "saved"). A different `from`/`to` or polygon is a new key.
- **Test polygon in the mockup:** `test_alps` (Aletsch region) is in the mockup's drop-down, labelled as a test, so the northern-summer path can be tried there. It is not a population and is not in the table.

## D-09 Delete a year, show the best single scene (owner request, 2026-11-03)
The request ("from the scene list, make it possible to delete individual scenes or show the best non-composite image") was read as:
- **Delete** (button per row of the scene table): takes that year out of the timelapse and the exports. It is reversible ("Show") and nothing is removed from the saved pictures. A year is one picture, so "scene" here means one year's frame, not one day inside a composite.
- **Best single scene** (button on composite rows, plus a checkbox "best single scene instead of composites" for all years): shows the least cloudy single acquisition day of that year instead of the composite. It is **not clear**, by definition (the composite exists because no day met the 2 % rule), so the caption, the table ("x % cloud") and the export band say how much cloud, shadow or no data the polygon has. The percentage is from the coarse scoring grid, so it is approximate.
- Both choices are kept per polygon in the browser (`localStorage`, key `gfs|<polygon hash>`), together with the saved pictures; the checkbox resets the per-year choices. Years with no picture are no longer frames in the player (they stay in the table and as chips).
- Composites now carry their best single scene, so `CACHE_VERSION` went to `v2` (older saved pictures are ignored).
- **Not built:** removing one day from inside a composite (e.g. one cloudy day that SCL missed) and re-computing it. That needs the day list per composite and a re-read; it is the other possible reading of the request (Q-11).
- Tested 2026-11-03 in headless Chromium on the Borneo box (2019 to 2021): single scene toggle (2020: 8.1 % cloud), delete (frames 3 to 2, GIF export had 2 frames), choice survives a reload, Show restores, the checkbox switches all three years (3.5, 8.1 and 9.9 % cloud).

## D-10 First year per polygon, not a constant (owner, 2026-11-03: "check Earth Search for the selected region, not only Borneo")
The fixed first year 2017 was only based on Borneo. Checked on 2026-11-03 with the Earth Search API (earliest Sentinel-2 L2A scene in a bbox, sorted ascending): Alps 2016-11-01, Iceland (Vatnajokull area) 2016-11-03, Borneo 2017-02-05, Patagonia 2017-01-13, Amazon 2017-01-03, Congo 2017-01-04, **Svalbard 2018-03-11**. So 2017 was too early for Svalbard (its summer 2017 has no scenes) and ignored late 2016 in the Alps.
Now the page asks Earth Search for the earliest scene over the polygon (`earliestScene`, one request, saved in the browser for 30 days) and offers years from the **first year whose season ends after that date** (Alps June to September: 2017, because the 2016 season was over before November 2016; Svalbard: 2018; a southern season December to March counts as the year it ends in). The page says which date it found. The tropics season search uses the same first year. A polygon with no scene at all (e.g. open ocean: tested at 0 N 30 W) gets the message "Earth Search has no Sentinel-2 L2A scene over this polygon" and stops.
Limits: "earliest scene" is not "complete coverage" (the first year can be sparse, e.g. Borneo 2017 had 37 scenes in the whole year); Earth Search's L2A archive before mid-2018 may be partly reprocessed data, not checked. Tested in headless Chromium for the Alps, Svalbard, Borneo, Patagonia and the ocean box (year dropdowns and notes); the full timelapse was not re-run for Svalbard or Patagonia.

## D-12 Mockup update, export progress, cleaner timelapse page, unreadable scenes (owner, 2026-11-03)

The PNG is instant and has none. The final message ("GIF saved.") replaces the bar.

This deliberately departs from the plain look of the live gfstool (owner: "here you can subtly make it look better").
- **Finding while testing the Turkey polygon:** some Earth Search items (5 of 96 over the Amanos area in Jul to Aug 2020) have asset links of the form `s3://sentinel-s2-l2a/...` (original JP2 files on a bucket that needs requester-pays credentials) instead of https COG links; the browser cannot read them and the first run logged failed reads. Such items are now **skipped** (both `scl` and `visual` must be https); if a year has only such scenes it says "n scenes found, but none in a format the browser can read". I did not check how common this is elsewhere or in which years it occurs.
- **Turkey run** (full 2017 to 2026, northern summer, first scene of the area 2016-11-02): ten years, all single clear scenes, 62 s fresh, GIF export with progress worked.
